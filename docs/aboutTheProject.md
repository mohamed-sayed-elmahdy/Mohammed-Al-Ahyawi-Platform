# Media Platform of Mohammed Al-Ihyawi

## Preliminary Vision for the Personal Media Platform Project

---

## Introduction

Mr. Mohammed Al-Ihyawi is not just a Local Guide who visits places and writes reviews.

After reviewing his content and years of experience, it's clear that there is a distinct message behind every visit and every review.

The work philosophy that recurs throughout his content revolves around:

* Media is a message
* The word is a trust
* Continuous improvement
* Experience, evaluate, improve

This is what makes the proposed project more than just a website or a profile page—it is a personal media platform that documents an extended journey of impact, evaluation, and improvement.

---

## Vision

To create an independent digital media platform that serves as the official digital home for media figure Mohammed Al-Ihyawi, bringing together his expertise, experiences, and reviews in one easily accessible place.

---

## Mission

To contribute to improving the quality of services and places by conveying the real experience objectively and credibly, with the aim of supporting continuous development and elevating the level of experiences offered to the public.

---

## Overview of Current Achievements

* Over 1,300 documented reviews and experiences.
* Ongoing activity on Google Maps.
* Presence on TikTok and Instagram.
* Visits and tours within the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.
* International experiences and tours including:

  * Egypt
  * United Arab Emirates
  * Turkey
  * Georgia

---

## Project Concept

Instead of experiences being scattered across multiple platforms, a unified platform is created that brings together all content, experiences, reviews, and stories in one place that carries the personal identity of media figure Mohammed Al-Ihyawi.

---

## Platform Pages

### Homepage

Includes:

* Platform message.
* Brief introduction.
* Numbers and achievements.
* Countries visited.
* Latest experiences and reviews.
* Links to official accounts.

---

### Tours Page

An interactive map displaying all places visited.

Visitors can browse:

* Cities.
* Countries.
* Restaurants.
* Cafés.
* Hotels.
* Tourist attractions.

With the ability to navigate directly to the details of each experience.

---

### Reviews Page

Display reviews organized within categories.

Such as:

* Restaurants.
* Cafés.
* Hotels.
* Shopping centers.
* Tourist destinations.

With sorting options by:

* Highest rated.
* Lowest rated.
* Newest.

---

### Stories Page

One of the most important pages of the project.

Because each visit is not just a review.

But a complete story including:

* First impression.
* Experience details.
* Positives.
* Observations.
* Lessons learned.
* Documented photos.

---

### Articles Page

A dedicated space for publishing articles, opinions, and analyses related to tourism, services, customer experiences, and continuous improvement.

---

### Request a Visit Page

Enables venues and establishments to send an official request to visit, experience, and evaluate the place.

The form includes:

* Establishment name.
* City.
* Type of activity.
* Contact information.
* Invitation details.

---

### Admin Dashboard

A dedicated dashboard for media figure Mohammed Al-Ihyawi that allows him to manage the platform easily and flexibly, so he can edit content and add whatever he wants at any time without complexity.

The dashboard includes:

* Adding, editing, and deleting experiences and reviews.
* Managing articles and stories.
* Uploading and organizing photos within each experience.
* Adding new tours and updating locations.
* Editing text and profile pages.
* Managing visit requests and responding to them.
* Controlling visibility of any content.
* Prioritizing or sorting content by date.
* Adding internal notes for each experience.
* Managing accounts and official links.
* Tracking basic content and visitor statistics.

This makes the platform flexible and continuously scalable, giving its owner complete freedom to update content and add anything new with ease.
