# Brand Identity

## Project Name

Media Platform for Journalist Mohammed Al Ahyawi


---

# Brand Vision

Create a premium personal media platform that documents authentic experiences, reviews, and journeys while reflecting professionalism, credibility, and continuous improvement.

---

# Brand Mission

Deliver trustworthy content that helps people make better decisions through real experiences, honest reviews, and meaningful storytelling.

---

# Brand Values

- Integrity
- Credibility
- Transparency
- Professionalism
- Continuous Improvement
- Authentic Experiences
- High Quality
- Simplicity

---

# Brand Personality

The platform should feel:

- Professional
- Elegant
- Premium
- Trustworthy
- Calm
- Confident
- Modern
- Informative

Avoid making the interface feel:

- Playful
- Cartoonish
- Overly colorful
- Noisy
- Corporate
- Generic

---

# Design Direction

Inspired by:

- Apple
- Bloomberg
- National Geographic
- Qatar Airways
- Luxury Hotel Websites

The overall experience should emphasize elegance, storytelling, and clarity.

# Imagery

Use real photography.

Avoid illustrations whenever possible.

Photography should communicate:

- Authenticity
- Hospitality
- Food
- Travel
- Cafes
- Retreats
- Real Experiences

---

# Motion

Smooth

Elegant

Purposeful

Avoid excessive animations.

Micro-interactions should reinforce quality rather than attract unnecessary attention.

---

# User Experience Principles

The user should immediately understand:

Who Mohammed Al Ahyawi is.

What his mission is.

Why his reviews are different.

How to explore experiences.

How to request a visit.

Every page should guide the user naturally toward the next action.
